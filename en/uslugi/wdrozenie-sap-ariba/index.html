SAP Ariba implementation

I lead SAP Ariba implementations from process and data decisions through configuration, integration, testing and launch. The scope follows the real procurement operating model, not a feature checklist.

01 / Problem

Process determines configuration.

Process decisions, data and roles establish scope. System configuration follows from them.

02 / Fit

Implementation needs decision owners.

Good fit
Business and technical teams can close decisions about process, data and integration.
Out of scope
The scope is expected to be a copied feature list without an agreed way of working.
