Public procurement advisory

I help structure procedure strategy, responsibilities, documents and decisions in public procurement projects. I work across procurement, technology and project governance, with a clear boundary around legal responsibility.

Procedure strategy, roles and criteria establish the working order. Documents record agreed decisions.

02 / Fit
