DOSSIER / ADVISORY 01

Procurement transformation

I structure the procurement operating model across decisions, roles, processes, data and technology. The deliverable is an agreed change design that can be implemented and governed.

01 / Problem

Operating model before tooling.

Transformation starts with decisions, accountability and data. Technology supports the agreed way of working.

02 / Fit

A clear decision on both sides.

Good fit
The organisation wants to agree its operating model and name the owners of change.
Out of scope
A ready answer is expected without access to data, process or decision-makers.

03 / Scope

Change-design register.

Model
Target Operating Model and role allocation.
Process
Source-to-pay, S2P and P2P.
Data
Spend analysis and ownership of definitions.
Decisions
Category strategy and governance.
Technology
Requirements derived from process and data.

04 / Method

Four decision closures.

01

Diagnosis

Current process, spend, roles and data.

02

Design

Target way of working and accountability.

03

Sequence

Order of change, dependencies and acceptance.

04

Governance

Decision owners and project-control model.
