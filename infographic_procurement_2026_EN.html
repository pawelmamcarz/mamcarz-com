Procurement process: reference model and scenario lenses

A compact workshop sheet for separating a process sequence from the choices made for a particular operating model.

This is a conceptual procurement operating model. Capability descriptions and scores are illustrative target-state assumptions, not claims about current product availability, legal compliance or a measured organisation.

Reference model

The sequence names seven handoffs. It does not prescribe a system, automation level or control design.

  1. Needs Definition
  2. Supplier Selection
  3. Order
  4. Goods Receipt
  5. Invoicing
  6. Matching
  7. Payment
