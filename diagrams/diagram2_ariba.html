Workshop vocabulary

Conceptual SAP procurement map

A static vocabulary for discussing a landscape. Each placement is a workshop hypothesis to verify for the organisation, not an implementation bill of materials or portfolio snapshot.

This is a conceptual procurement operating model. Capability descriptions and scores are illustrative target-state assumptions, not claims about current product availability, legal compliance or a measured organisation.

Product names are model labels. Verify availability, scope and licensing for the relevant SAP landscape.
